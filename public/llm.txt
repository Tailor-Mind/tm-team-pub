# TailorMind — Talent

Hiring site for senior AI-empowered full-stack developers, remote, based in Peru.
Bilingual: English (/en/) and Spanish (/es/).

## Key pages
- /en/ , /es/                  — landing
- /en/role/ , /es/role/        — job description (Senior Full-stack, AI-empowered)
- /en/process/ , /es/process/  — 5-stage hiring process, ~2–3 weeks total
- /en/flow/ , /es/flow/        — Mermaid flow diagrams of the process
- /en/assessment/ , /es/assessment/ — autonomy + criterio assessment overview
- /en/apply/ , /es/apply/      — application form
- /en/privacy/ , /es/privacy/  — privacy notice (Peru Ley 29733)

## What we measure
- Criterio: judgment under uncertainty (Kahneman/Klein, Tetlock, Duke, Simon).
- Autonomy: drives ambiguous work to clarity, async-first.
- AI fluency: uses Claude as a force multiplier.
- Engineering rigor: full-stack, tests, docs.
